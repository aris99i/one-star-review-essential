An entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-0xb3e689-one-star-review-of-something-essential-c7234629) "One-Star Review of Something Essential" — a single-line, deadpan one-star review of an everyday essential everyone relies on.

## The review

> One star. Shows up late, leaves before my alarm, and spends all night replaying conversations from 2019.

104 characters. Filed in earnest against sleep: it is late, it quits
early, and its night shift is spent replaying conversations from years
ago that nobody asked to hear again. No winking, no exclamation marks —
the sting arrives in the last few words, exactly where a one-star review
should put it. Sleep was picked over the sun, gravity and Mondays
because its grievance is the most private one: everyone has lain awake
being annoyed by their own memory, and nobody has ever written it down
as a complaint against sleep itself.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes the review to
`/output/entry.txt`, then checks the house gate itself: one line, 1–160
characters, or it refuses to ship.
